# Reading guide

* **Ten minutes.** Section I (what the object is and the three questions),
  Section IV (the impossibility theorem and its sharp constant), Section XIV
  (conclusion).
* **If you build pipelines.** Section IV.C–D (what a lossy ledger costs, and what
  quantisation costs), Section XI (what to carry between shards, and when to use
  the midpoint instead), Algorithm 3 (planning a certified pipeline).
* **If you care about the mathematics.** Section III (the algebra), Section V
  (temperature calculus), Section VI (coarse-graining and the chain rule),
  Appendix A (proofs A.1–A.10).
* **If you care about the quantum claim.** Section VIII, in particular
  Theorem 11 (dephasing is a contraction), Corollary 1 (Helstrom floor) and
  Theorem 12 (no converse).
* **If you are reviewing.** Section IX (prior-art table, the one claim offered as
  new, and what would falsify each statement), Section XII (nine limitations),
  Section XIII (what "reproducible" means here).

Every number in the text is reproducible from `results/*.json`; the map from a
quoted scalar to its file is Table B.1, and the experiment roster is Table 7.
The one-page novelty assessment in Arabic is
`publication/OTAZ_III_Novelty_Assessment_AR.pdf`.
